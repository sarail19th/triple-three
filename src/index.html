How to play

  • On a computer, hover a tile to preview the move (the result shows faintly, merged items are outlined) and click to place the item shown in Place. On a touch screen, tap to preview and tap again to place. Turn Preview off to place with one tap.
  • Connect 3 or more identical items (up, down, left, right) and all of them merge into one of the next tier on the tile you tapped (4 or 5 pay more points and coins, nothing is left over):
    🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳 → 🛖 → 🏠 → 🏡 → 🏰 → 🏯
  • 🐻 Bears wander one step each turn. Box one in so it cannot move and it becomes a 🪦. Three connected 🪦 merge into ⛪, three ⛪ into 🕌, three 🕌 into a 💰 treasure: tap it to open it for coins.
  • 💎 The crystal is a wildcard: it turns into whatever makes the best merge. If it cannot merge yet it waits, and merges as soon as a group of 3 is possible next to it.
  • 🪙 Coins come from merges. Spend them in the shop: buy 🌱 🌿 🌳 as your current item (the old one moves to Next), or use the 🪤 bear trap to kill a bear on the spot (it turns into a 🪦), or the 🔨 hammer to remove any placed item. Shop actions are free.
  • The Reserve tray (top left) stores the current item for later (free swap). Undo takes back your last move.
  • The game ends when the board is full and you cannot afford a hammer.
