How to play

  • Tap an empty tile to place the item shown in Place.
  • Connect 3 or more identical items (up, down, left, right) and they merge into the next tier on the tile you tapped:
    🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳 → 🛖 → 🏠 → 🏡 → 🏰 → 🏯
  • 🐻 Bears wander one step each turn. Box one in so it cannot move and it becomes a 🪦. Three connected 🪦 merge into ⛪, three ⛪ into 🕌.
  • 💎 The crystal becomes whatever item makes the best merge where you drop it.
  • Swap stores the current item for later (free). Undo takes back your last move.
  • The game ends when the board is full.
