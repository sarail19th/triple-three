How to play

  • On a computer, hover a tile to preview the move (the result shows faintly, merged items are outlined) and click to place the item shown in Place. On a touch screen, tap to preview and tap again to place. Turn Preview off to place with one tap.
  • Connect 3 or more identical items (up, down, left, right) and all of them merge into one of the next tier on the tile you tapped (4 or 5 pay more points and coins, nothing is left over):
    🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳 → 🏕️ → 🛖 → 🏠 → 🏘️ → 🏫 → 🏥 → 🏬 → 💵 cash: tap it to collect coins.
  • 🐻 Bears wander one step each turn. Box one in so it cannot move and it becomes a 🪦 (bears sharing a pocket that still has room are safe). Three connected 🪦 merge into ⛪, three ⛪ into 🕌, three 🕌 into a 💰 treasure: tap it to open it for coins.
  • 🦊 The fox never walks: every turn it warps to a random empty tile. Walls cannot trap it. Only the 🪤 trap, or a completely full board, turns it into a 🪦.
  • 💎 The crystal is a wildcard: it turns into whatever makes the best merge (buildings, 🪦, ⛪ or 🕌). If it cannot merge yet it waits, and merges as soon as a group of 3 is possible next to it.
  • 🪙 Coins come from merges. Spend them in the shop: buy an item as your current one (the old one moves to Next). You can buy 🌱 🌿 🌳, and any higher tier once you have built one. Stock is limited (the ×N badge): after it runs out the price doubles with every purchase, and score milestones restock it.
  • 🪤 kills a bear or fox on the spot, 🔨 removes any placed item, and ⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️ buy land: one more row or column on that side (each side once, dearer every time).
  • The Reserve tray (top left) stores the current item for later (free swap). Undo takes back your last move for a few coins.
  • The game ends when the board is full and you cannot afford a hammer.
