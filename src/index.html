How to play

  • On a computer, hover a tile to preview the move (the result shows faintly, merged items are outlined) and click to place the item shown in Place. On a touch screen, tap to preview and tap again to place. Turn Preview off to place with one tap.
  • Connect 3 or more identical items (up, down, left, right) and all of them merge into one of the next tier on the tile you tapped (4 or 5 pay more points and coins, nothing is left over):
    🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳 → 🏕️ → 🛖 → 🏠 → 🏘️ → 🏫 → 🏥 → 🏬 → 💵 cash: tap it to collect coins.
  • 🐻 Bears wander one step each turn. Box one in so it cannot move and it becomes a 🪦 (bears sharing a pocket that still has room are safe). Three connected 🪦 merge into ⛪, three ⛪ into 🕌, three 🕌 into a 💰 treasure: tap it to open it for coins.
  • 🐻💢 Wild bears (pick it in New): a bear that cannot step but is not sealed in gets angry (💢, then 💢💢) and on the third turn mauls a 🌱🌿🌳🏕️🛖 next to it one tier down (a 🌱 is destroyed, which opens an escape hole), then starts over. Houses and everything above are safe. In a small pen (4 to 6 tiles) holding 2 bears and 2 free tiles, a cub is sometimes born 🍼.
  • 🍀 Lucky drops: once you have built a higher tier, the next item is sometimes a free 🏕️ 🛖 or 🏠 (a tier below your best, never above a house). Most often on Easy, less on Standard and Medium, never on Hard.
  • 🗺️ Maps (also in New): Easy is a plain field, Standard starts with a few plants, Medium has a river across the field, Hard has random ponds. Blue tiles are water: nothing can be placed, merged, moved or smashed there, and animals cannot enter it. Use the 🌉 bridge tool (up to 5, dearer each time) on a water tile that has land on both sides, up/down or left/right (never a corner or the middle of wide water): it becomes a road that bears can walk over, but nothing can be built on it.
  • 🦊 The fox never walks: every turn it warps to a random empty tile (never into a hole it could not get out of). Corner it, though: when walls and items close it in on every side (or you fill the last free tile of its box) it becomes a 🪦, and so does a fox on a full board. The 🪤 trap kills it anywhere.
  • 💎 The crystal is a wildcard: it turns into whatever makes the best merge (any building tier up to 🏬 which makes 💵 cash, 🪦, ⛪ or 🕌 which makes a 💰 treasure). If no tier fits it waits as a wildcard, and three 💎 touching each other merge into a 💰 treasure on the tile you tapped.
  • 🪙 Coins come from merges. Spend them in the shop: buy an item as your current one (the old one moves to Next). You can buy 🌱 🌿 🌳, and any higher tier once you have built one. Stock is limited (the ×N badge): after it runs out the price doubles with every purchase, and score milestones restock it.
  • 🪤 kills a bear or fox on the spot, 🔨 removes any placed item, 🚚 moves one item (not animals) to an empty tile, merging it there like a placed item, and ⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️ buy land: one more row or column on that side (each side once, dearer every time).
  • Tap the ⇄ button between Place and Next (or the Next item itself) to swap them (free, as often as you like). The Reserve slot (left of Place) stores the current item for later (free swap). Undo takes back your last move for a few coins.
  • The game ends when the board is full and you cannot afford a hammer.
